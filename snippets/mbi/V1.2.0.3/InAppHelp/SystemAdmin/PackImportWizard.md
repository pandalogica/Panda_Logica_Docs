# Import an accelerator pack

Choose the complete pack ZIP, map its library to your environment and review the items to import. Category entitlements and your tier's dashboard/layout limits still apply.

## Choose the import behaviour

**Add only** adds missing items and keeps existing items and their current categories. Re-importing a newer pack in this mode does not move existing queries.

**Update pack-owned items** can update eligible items owned by the pack, including their category. Review the preview before importing. Customer-owned items remain subject to the existing ownership checks.

## Find and select items

Use **Search** to find queries, dashboards or layouts. **Show selected items only** helps review your choices. Filtering does not clear selections: checked items hidden by the filter remain selected. Category and document-group checkboxes apply to all their children, including filtered-out items. Clear the search to see the whole list.

Check the selected totals, Outcome column, summary and final preview. **Add**, **Update** and **Keep existing** explain the expected result. **Check in preview** means the selection page cannot yet determine the outcome.

## Suggested groups and roles

Review the supplied names and choose whether to create or match them to existing groups and roles. If the pack supplies no role suggestions, the Roles tab says so. BPCS 1.0.42 restores its 23 authored role suggestions. Suggested roles do not add query access beyond the pack's access recommendations.
