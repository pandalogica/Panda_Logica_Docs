# Master reports and detail tabs

Choose a master record, such as an item or customer, to show its header information and related detail reports. Detail tabs use the report's display caption. The report's internal identifier and data relationship are unchanged.

For Customer Master, the supporting tabs include **Shipments** and **AR invoice lines**. These reports may be hidden from the main menu because they are included as dependencies of the master report. Item Master uses the same approach for its linked detail reports.
